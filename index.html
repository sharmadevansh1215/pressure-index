How it works
  1. Pressure = leverage. A win-probability model scores how far each ball can swing the match; High pressure is at least 2× an average ball (7.5% of balls).
  2. Value over the league. Runs added (batters) or saved (bowlers) versus the league in the same situation.
  3. Shrinkage. Usual level and pressure deviation are pooled with Bayesian shrinkage; pressure-only “clutch” does not repeat between halves of the data (r ≈ −0.04), usual level does (0.31 / 0.37).
  4. Consistency and stability. Share of pressure matches beating the league, then rank averaged over 300 match-level bootstraps.

Expected value (θ) = runs above league per 100 balls in the same situation. P(top 10) = share of resamples in the top 10. Tier: A direct fit for RR’s gaps · B strong, partly duplicative · C watch-list.
